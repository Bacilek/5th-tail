## PART A. Historical backstory (before Violet's first day)

### A1. Dragons, riders and the First Six
- Navarre's founding myth: the provinces were joined under the shelter of the first bonded riders. The Scribes' curator (Markham) presents this version in the books (ER, Kingdom of Navarre). **[FW]**
- The dragons are described as having claimed the western lands, and gryphons the central ones, in the aftermath of the Great War (EW snippet) (?).
- "The First Six" are the founding riders. In **[IF]** Violet learns they carved the wardstones and left journals (Warrick's, Lyra's and others) kept in a royal-blood-only vault under the Archives. Warrick's journal lies about how to activate the wards; Lyra's journal has the true method (WP; RFB). **[IF]**
- The true method (**[IF]**): the wardstone must be fed magic and fire from a dragon of every den. Lyra's text indicates seven dens, not six; Andarna is the seventh (irid) (WP). Violet first misreads the key phrase as "iron rain / blood" and later corrects it to "iron flame / breath", meaning dragons, not riders, power the stones (RFB). **[IF]**
- Six dens named in the narrative are not listed in the sources I could read (?). MEM only.
- Dragon history is hidden: irids, the seventh den, once existed on the Continent. In **[OS]** they say they left Andarna's egg as a test for humanity and judge that humanity failed (WP; RC). **[OS]**
- Andarna waited about 650 years in her egg before hatching (IM) (?, check against the books).

### A2. The Great War and unification
- The Great War is placed "more than six centuries" before the books and ended with the unification of Navarre; its enemy was General Daramor and his forces (EW snippet). After it the Barrens were abandoned (EW snippet) (?).
- The Battle of Gianfar is named as a pivotal battle of that war, where the first cross-bolt lethal to dragons was used (EW snippet) (?). SH dates it to "1 AU" instead; the sources disagree.
- Fan timeline (SH, low trust): Navarre forms from the provinces Luceras, Morraine, Elsum, Deaconshire and Calldyr around 1 AU; Tyrrendor is the last province to swear loyalty, under the first wards. ER agrees only that Tyrrendor was last to pledge to King Reginalt and later the only province to attempt secession.
- The Unification forbade travel to the southern isles (SH, low trust). In **[OS]** the isles are rediscovered by a Navarrian expedition; Violet's father researched them (WP; RC). **[OS]**
- Augustine Melgren's signet (seeing battle outcomes) is described as a turning point in how Navarre fights wars (ER, Navarre page). Melgren is also the general in Violet's time, with his dragon Codagh (BSR). Whether this is the same man or a title or namesake is (?); in the books the general appears to be a living man of the current era, so treat the ER line as lore about "Melgren" the signet-type, not a founding figure (?).

### A3. Wards
- The wards guard Navarre and keep venin out, which is why most citizens consider venin mythical (WP). **[FW]**
- Side effect (**[FW]/[IF]**): magic fades inside wards for some; gryphon riders cannot channel (wield) inside Navarre's wards. In **[OS]** Violet's squad alters the wardstone runes at Basgiath so gryphon riders can wield there, which is the bargain made with Viscount Tecarus and Queen Maraya (RC; WP). **[OS]**
- The Aretia wards (Tyrrendor's valley) were never fully active. Violet raises them briefly in **[IF]** (WP) and Andarna sustains them; after she gives power to Basgiath's stone they are predicted to last about six more months (WP; ER). **[IF]/[OS]**
- Basgiath's wardstone falls in **[IF]** when Jack Barlowe, now venin, uses his dragon Baide's blood to extinguish the fire on the stone (RFB). Brennan later mends it and Lilith dies powering it (WP). **[IF]**

### A4. The Tyrrish Rebellion
- Names: Tyrrendor calls it the apostasy; Navarre calls it the secession (ER).
- Leader: Fen Riorson, Xaden's father. He was captured at the Battle of Aretia and later executed (ER). **[FW]** General Lilith Sorrengail (Violet's mother) is the figure who executed him in Violet's family story (WP: "executed by her mother"; BSR). Whether Lilith personally carried it out or ordered it is (?). Violet's brother Brennan is believed to have died in the rebellion's final battle; actually he survived at Aretia and was hidden in the valley for about six years (RFB; WP). **[FW]/[IF]**
- Timing: the rebellion's end was roughly six years before Violet starts college. RFB says Aretia was burned six years before **[IF]** opens; IM says "about twenty years" (probably in error). Fan timeline SH gives 594 AU; Reddit snippet places **[FW]** itself at 633 AU, and ER says the rebellion began 627 years after Tyrrendor joined. These do not agree (?). Best guess: rebellion peak and Aretia roughly 6 years before the books, in the 620s AU (?).
- Executions: all rebellion leaders were put to death in public with their children forced to watch; SH counts 107 leaders (?), ER counts 107 marked children, the best-supported figure.
- Marked children: 107 children, Xaden the oldest, were given swirled marks ("rebellion relics") on their arms. Early belief: Melgren's dragon Codagh burned them with the marks. ER now reports the marks were an unintended effect of a rune made by Colonel Mairi, Liam Mairi (?), and Sloane's mother, which was meant to blunt the signet of the executing dragon's rider (ER; flagged by ER as not yet updated, so uncertain). The marked ones were sent to be state wards at Basgiath and had to prove loyalty in the Riders Quadrant (IM; BSR).
- Xaden's 107 back scars: he took formal responsibility for each marked child as part of the bargain (BSR). **[FW]**
- Result: the Treaty of Aretia (EW snippet): Tyrrendor controls its own military, under named commanders; the treaty also gives Xaden the Duke of Tyrrendor title in some sources. In **[OS]** the king officially pardons the Aretia rebels and names Xaden Duke (RC). **[OS]** The conflict between those two statements is (?).
- Aftermath: Aretia is a ruined valley city that the rebels, led secretly by Brennan, rebuild. The marked ones form Fourth Wing's core and a smuggling network with Poromish gryphon riders. **[FW]**

### A5. Navarre and Poromiel
- Poromiel is the neighbouring kingdom, long at odds with Navarre; a trade agreement had been brokered through Resson, a border town. After the war Navarre closed its borders yet allowed Poromiel citizens to live under its wards for a limited time (ER; WP).
- Poromiel's flying force is gryphon riders (fliers). Venin attack Poromiel, and only Navarrian-forged weapons can kill them (WP). **[FW]**
- Xaden has secretly supplied Poromish gryphon riders with weapons (BSR). **[FW]**
- Poromiel's province Krovla borders Tyrrendor. Viscount Tecarus (of Krovla) holds a luminary. His niece Cat was betrothed to Xaden, an engagement that ended when Tecarus never meant to share the luminary (RFB; WP). **[IF]**
- The Krovlan uprising is a history item in Violet's father's papers (RC). **[OS]**
- Late events: venin kill Queen Maraya of Poromiel at Suniva; Tecarus becomes king (RC). **[OS]**

## PART B. Event list per book

### B1. FOURTH WING [FW] (approx. July to the following summer)

Year framing (ER Riders Quadrant): candidates arrive in July; first-year survival training; Gauntlet about two months later; Presentation, then Threshing two days after; Reunification Day on July 1; graduation July 10.

1. **Late spring, Violet's home / Basgiath.** Violet, trained as a scribe, is ordered by her mother, General Lilith Sorrengail, to enter the Riders Quadrant. She has had only about six months of combat training. Her sister Mira helps pack, gives her armor of dragon scale and balance-supporting boots, and warns her against Xaden Riorson (BSR; RFB). Her health condition is a fragile skeleton and joints; fan recaps name it as Ehlers-Danlos-like (RFB) (?).
2. **Conscription Day (early July), Basgiath.** Candidates gather and Violet meets Rhiannon Matthias; she notices Xaden before she knows who he is. A lecture rehearses Navarre's history (BSR).
3. **The Parapet (same day).** Candidates cross a narrow, rain-slick stone span high over a gorge; ER says about 15% die. A candidate (named Dylan in one recap) falls. Violet swaps boots with Rhiannon to avoid a slip, is harassed by Jack Barlowe, who tries to push her, and she threatens him with a dagger at the far end (BSR; RFB). **Set piece.**
4. **Entry to the Quadrant.** Violet reunites with Dain Aetos, a second-year, who wants to send her to the Scribes. She refuses and is assigned to Fourth Wing under Xaden (BSR).
5. **First days.** Dragons are shown to intimidate candidates; some who flee are burned (BSR). Classes begin: Battle Brief (Professors Devera and Markham), sparring, history and others. Violet impresses Devera by deducing that an attack on a high-altitude village could only work if attackers knew the wards would fail (WP / search snippet). **Set piece: Battle Brief.**
6. **Rhiannon and Violet trade tutoring.** Violet teaches history, Rhiannon teaches fighting (BSR).
7. **Brennan's journal.** A journal of Brennan's helps Violet learn the sparring schedule (BSR). She begins secretly brewing poisons from night-gathered bark.
8. **Night meeting of the marked ones.** Violet overhears Xaden and other rebel children and learns about their mutual distrust of Melgren, whose battle-foresight stops working when marked ones gather. Xaden catches her and lets her live, creating a debt (BSR). **Set piece.**
9. **Early sparring challenges.** Violet wins several by poisoning; at her sixth challenge Xaden exposes it (RFB: BSR says he warns her), then trains her and tells her to stop hiding (BSR; RFB).
10. **Attack by Imogen.** A sparring match with Imogen, a marked rebel with a grudge against Lilith, injures Violet; healers fix her (RFB) (?). Imogen later trains Violet at Xaden's request (BSR).
11. **Dain's transfer attempt.** Dain keeps pushing for her to move to the Scribes (RFB).
12. **Challenge duels and the Fourth Wing/Third Wing rivalry.** Amber Mavis (Third Wing leader, in BSR; Fourth Wing cadet, in RFB) is introduced (conflict) (?).
13. **The Gauntlet (about 2 months in, early autumn).** A multi-obstacle climbing and survival test; a squadmate, Aurelie, falls. Violet survives by improvising with ropes and daggers; Amber demands punishment, Violet cites the Codex and is cleared (BSR; RFB). **Set piece.**
14. **Presentation (about Sept 29, per one fan wiki) (?).** Riders walk before the dragons. Pryor and Luca die in dragon fire. Violet notices a small golden dragon (BSR; search snippet).
15. **Eve of Threshing.** She overhears Jack, Tynan and Oren planning to kill the golden dragon as a weak link (BSR).
16. **Threshing (Oct 1).** Violet fights the three; Xaden watches, forbidden to interfere. She stabs Tynan, knocks out Oren; Tairn, a huge black dragon, lands, burns Tynan and bonds with her. Andarna, the golden feathertail, bonds to her as well, the first double bond (BSR). **Set piece.**
17. **Aftermath.** Leaders are alarmed; the Dragon Council (the dragons) allows it (RFB). Dain kisses Violet; she feels nothing. She learns Tairn is mated to Sgaeyl, Xaden's dragon, tethering her life to his (BSR; WP).
18. **Rider's room and leathers.** New riders get private rooms and leathers (ER).
19. **Flight training.** She struggles on Tairn; Imogen coaches (BSR).
20. **Archives.** Jesinia the scribe-archivist cannot find the lost book of fables; Violet learns of Jeremiah, an illegal intinnsic (mind-reader) killed by a professor (BSR).
21. **Assassination attempt in her room.** Tairn wakes her; attackers led by Oren break in, against the Codex. Andarna lets Violet freeze time; Xaden arrives and kills most of them; one escapes (BSR; RFB). **Set piece: assassination attempt.**
22. **Revelation about Andarna.** Tairn and Sgaeyl admit Andarna is a juvenile feathertail whose power can accidentally transfer. Amber is revealed as the one who let attackers in and is executed by the dragons (BSR; RFB). Dain, who can read recent memories by touching a face, doubts Violet; Tairn shares her memories with others to prove her case (BSR).
23. **Liam as escort.** Xaden assigns Liam Mairi to protect her; Liam tells her about the 107 marked ones (BSR).
24. **Archive discovery.** Violet and Liam find evidence of an attack missing from the Battle Brief record (BSR).
25. **Channeling.** Tairn lets Violet draw on his power; she is also affected by his emotions, e.g. when he and Sgaeyl mate. Violet and Xaden share a kiss, then stop (BSR; RFB).
26. **Rhiannon's signet.** Rhiannon can summon items (BSR).
27. **Duel with Jack.** Violet defeats him with oranges, to which he is allergic (BSR). Xaden then trains her himself and gives her custom daggers (RFB).
28. **Squad competition and theft.** Her squad steals a map of outposts and supply routes from Lilith's office and wins, earning a trip to Montserrat (RFB) (?).
29. **Montserrat.** The squad visits Mira's outpost; gryphons attack and Mira survives; Mira gives Violet the fable book (BSR). **Set piece.**
30. **Saddle.** Xaden and Tairn give Violet a custom saddle (BSR).
31. **War Games, capture-the-flag stage.** Fourth Wing competes to retrieve an egg. Jack targets Violet's squad. Violet saves Liam as he falls from his dragon Deigh. She goes after Jack, unlocks her lightning signet and strikes his tower; he dies (BSR; RFB). **Set piece: War Games.**
32. **Aftermath.** Violet is shaken, throws daggers in her room. Xaden visits and shows his back: 107 scars, one per marked child (BSR; RFB).
33. **Father's note.** A note from her father in the fable book warns of history being erased and urges the right choice (BSR).
34. **Training with Professor Carr** to control lightning (RFB).
35. **Reunification Day / anniversary.** Basgiath marks the end of the rebellion; Violet comforts Xaden. Violet crosses the parapet barefoot in dress uniform to reach him and says she loves him (BSR; RFB). In ER's calendar this is July 1 (?). Dates conflict with a winter placement in BSR (?).
36. **Alarm.** Garrick interrupts them; the "attack" is the final War Games test. Xaden's squad is sent to the outpost at Athebyne (RFB; WP).
37. **Journey to Athebyne.** Violet goes along since her dragon cannot be parted from Xaden's for long (BSR).
38. **Lake meeting.** Near Athebyne they meet Poromish gryphon riders, including Syrena. Xaden has been supplying them with weapons; Liam and Imogen were in on it. Violet feels betrayed (BSR; WP). **Set piece.**
39. **The truth about venin.** The venin and wyvern from her book of fables are real; the dragons knew (BSR).
40. **Colonel Aetos's trap.** He learned of the trips through Dain's memory-reading and left orders for the group to survive if they could (BSR; WP).
41. **Choice to defend Resson.** The group chooses to defend the village rather than retreat (BSR). **Set piece: Resson.**
42. **Battle.** Wyvern (two-legged, dragon-like) and venin. Liam's dragon Deigh is killed and Liam dies with him (BSR).
43. **Violet's fight.** She kills venin with lightning, defeats the last in hand-to-hand using a venin-killing dagger, is stabbed with poisoned steel, and wyvern controlled by the venin die with it (BSR; WP).
44. **Xaden POV: evacuation to Aretia.** Survivors carry Violet to the hidden valley (BSR).
45. **Wake-up in Aretia (three days later, RFB).** Brennan, alive, heals her and welcomes her to the rebellion (BSR; RFB). Cliffhanger.

Consequences at end of **[FW]**: the rebels are exposed to Violet; Lilith demotes/sidelines Colonel Aetos later (WP); Violet breaks with Dain in **[IF]**.
