## 1. Dragons

### 1.1 Colours (seven) and temperament
Six colours were long believed; the seventh, **Irid**, was revealed in Onyx Storm [AUD, ER].

| Colour | Temperament / role | Game visual or mechanical hook |
|---|---|---|
| Black | Rarest, cunning, most discerning; largest. Tairn and Codagh. None hatched for about a century before the series [II] | Biggest model, dark glossy scales, lightning/storm VFX (but signet is rider's, not colour-bound) |
| Blue | Large, ruthless; blue Daggertail is the rarest and deadliest combination; Sgaeyl [AUD, II] | Navy-blue, shadow VFX |
| Green | Most rational, steady link to magic; strong siege dragons (esp. clubtails); green dragons are said to hold the Basgiath wards [AUD, II] | Approach etiquette: lower eyes, wait for approval, then stand your ground [II] |
| Red | Quickest temper; never meet their eyes; red Scorpiontails specifically have an approach rule (left and front) [AUD, II] | Orange-red glow; aggression |
| Orange | Unpredictable, can help or hurt a rider [AUD]; fans link orange to unsavoury riders (Jack, Varrish) but Marbh (Brennan) is orange [II] | |
| Brown | Loyal; do not show fear [AUD]. Lilith's Aimsir, Ridoc's Aotrom, Garrick's Chradh | |
| Irid | Extremely rare; iridescent; looks black with a purple shimmer; can appear in different colours and use magic others cannot [AUD, ER Andarna] | Shader: black base with purple/oil-slick shimmer |
| Gold (hatchlings) | Juvenile "feathertails" are golden until they mature into den colour [ER, II] | Baby model, small, no claws |

### 1.2 Tail types (six)
Club, scorpion, sword, morningstar, dagger, feather (juvenile only) [ER, AUD]. **Conflicting claims:** [ER dragons page] ties tails to colour (black=morningstar, blue=dagger, green=club, red=sword/scorpion, orange=dagger, brown=club, irid=scorpion); [AUD] says tails are not tied to colour and develop by dragon's choice/need; observed pairs contradict the colour rule (e.g. blue Clubtail Molvic, brown Swordtail Aotrom, green Daggertail Feirge, orange Scorpiontail Baide, black Swordtail Codagh). Use [AUD]: tail independent of colour.
Tail visuals: morningstar = spiked ball, dagger = knife spikes, sword = long blade edge, club = blunt mass (siege), scorpion = venomous barbs [II].

### 1.3 Size and anatomy
- About 25 feet tall (estimate, [ER] but source unclear (?)). Tairn is the second largest in service; Codagh the largest [AUD, II].
- Four legs, wings, fire breath, claws. Hatchlings/feathertails: no claws, no fire, too small to carry a rider [II, ER].
- Wyverns have two legs and are larger than dragons [ER].

### 1.4 Hatching, Dreamless Sleep, growth
- Dragons hatch in lineage-specific hatching grounds ("dens"): Vale near Basgiath (originally green); Steelridge Mountains (green, Uaineloidsig); thermal valley above Riorson House (black, Dubhmadinn); limestone cliffs by the Dunness River (red); Northern Esbens (orange, uncertain); near Cordyn (blue, before the Great War) [ER, II].
- Only the den head knows what colour a hatchling will become [ER].
- **Dreamless Sleep**: a period of rapid growth to finish maturing; length varies by breed; dragons wake hungry. ER says only four facts are known. Andarna enters it in Iron Flame and wakes with black-looking iridescent scales and a scorpiontail [ER, WP].
- Egg left in Navarre by the irids about 650 years ago (?) as a test of humanity ("criterion") [fan profile, BSR]. The eggs: six eggs stolen from the hatching grounds at the end of Onyx Storm; who took them is unresolved [BSR, ER]. Unnbriel's queen demanded twelve eggs (two per breed) for aid [ER]; Unnbriel has no magic, thus odd.
- Each new bond a dragon forms is stronger than the last [ER].

### 1.5 Bonding, burning, rejection
- Threshing: the dragon chooses; may bond, ignore, or kill the candidate. A dragon must get approval from its den head before Threshing [AUD, II].
- Rider dies if the dragon dies ("burnout" in II also names death from overusing the signet). Dragon survives rider's death usually; rarely grief death (three recorded cases) [ER, AUD].
- Mind-link: telepathic speech and emotional sense of distance; dragons talk only with the bonded rider [ER, AUD]. Dragons reveal their full name only to their rider [ER].
- Mated dragons: lifelong; the strongest bond; cannot be separated beyond about 3 days without declining; their riders are entangled (Tairn and Sgaeyl, Violet and Xaden) [ER, II].
- Gaelic naming: Tairneanach (thunder), Andarnaurram (second honour), Sgaeyl (shadow), Aimsir (weather), Teine (fire), Codagh (war), Cath (battle), Feirge (anger), Deigh (ice), Aotrom (light), Baide (herd), Claidh (cemetery), Fuil (blood), Gleann (glen), Sliseag (slice), Smachd (discipline) [ER AUD-like table]. Fans note names hint at signets, unconfirmed [II].
- Kinship rule: dragons are forbidden from bonding relatives of former riders; breaking it risks madness, or a second signet [AUD].

### 1.6 The Empyrean (dragon government) and etiquette
- The Empyrean is the dragons' governing body; humans do not know its proceedings or have any say in dragon law; strict hierarchy with elders respected most [ER].
- Dragons don't tolerate humans they haven't chosen; approach rules by colour above; riders must show respect; riders do not choose dragons.
- Irids are a separate seventh den that left (or watches) in the isles; Leothan and Dasyn (irids) appear [ER Onyx Storm guide]. Whether the Empyrean includes irids: unknown.

### 1.7 Feathertails and Andarna
- Feathertails are juveniles of all dragons, golden, and can give a human their power as a gift; it drains them and may burn out the rider; so they are considered unsuitable for bonding. Dragons keep this secret to protect them from hunters [AUD, ER].
- Andarna: the first baby dragon seen by humans, first to bond as hatchling; can briefly stop time as a direct gift (Fourth Wing); later irid with scorpiontail; in Onyx Storm the irids judge her too warlike and she leaves with Leothan, then returns [ER, BSR].
- Conflict: sources call her "gold feathertail" (FW) / "black Morningstar-like"? Only Tairn is Morningstar; irid = scorpiontail per ER.

### 1.8 Named dragons (colour/tail, rider)

## 2. Gryphons and fliers
- Half lion, half mount/eagle with dark brown feathers, razor beaks and claws; about a foot taller than Xaden; weak at high altitude [ER]. Seven gryphons = a "drift" [ER]. Humans are **fliers**, who wear brown leathers.
- Fliers are to gryphons what riders are to dragons. Gryphons die with their fliers (unlike dragons) [ER read-along snippet].
- They can channel power to fliers but cannot power the wards and cannot channel inside Navarrian wards (so Poromiel's fliers are weak there) [ER]; Violet's group modified the Basgiath wardstone so fliers can wield [BSR]. Source conflict: another read-along says gryphons draw from the same power as dragons.
- First known gryphon attack: 1 AU [ER].
- Named: Dajalair (Daja, brown-and-white, Maren Zina), Cibbelair (Luella, dead), Kiralair (Kira, Catriona Cordella), Sila (Trager) [ER, II].
- Gryphon fliers gave signets: Catriona (heighten emotions), Mauren (mind work) [ER Onyx Storm guide].

## 3. Wyverns
- Grey, two-legged, larger than dragons, mane of blade-like feathers, poison-tipped barbed tail, rotted teeth, taste for human flesh; contain runes [ER].
- Fire colours: blue (base), green (faster breed), cherry red (?), seen at Zolya/Resson [ER, BG].
- Made by venin, which channel into them; the wyvern dies when its maker dies [ER, II]. Collective consciousness with the maker [ER].
- Kill methods: kill the venin; Ridoc froze its liquids so it shattered [BSR]. Wyverns cannot be drained [ER].

## 4. Venin (Dark Wielders)
- Folklore creatures from the Fables of the Barren, revealed as real [ER].
- Made by drawing magic straight from the earth even once (Initiate), irreversible, addictive, "kills the soul piece by piece"; no cure (per irids) [BG, BSR]. Anyone can do it; riders and fliers find it easier.
- Ranks: Initiate (red rings around eyes appear and fade), Asim (red eye colour, swollen veins when angry), Sage (permanently red eyes, vein spiderweb to temples, wears robes), Maven (generals, never captured, appearance unknown) [ER, BG].
- Look: gaunt faces, sharp cheekbones, thin lips, bright red eyes, gnarled hands; purple robes (ER) or red fighting leathers and staves (BG); Theophanie is silver-haired [ER, WP].
- Powers: fire, pain, gravity, shielding, destruction, fireproofing, adaptation to fighting style, storms (Theophanie), dream manipulation (Sage Brewyn/Berwyn) [BG, ER]. They can summon venin (Sage) [BG]. Cannot drain wyverns.
- Limits: must touch ground to channel; drained ground (stands made of drained stone) blocks them; lose powers inside Navarre's wards [ER, BG]; drained alloy daggers kill them but weaken after a few strikes [BG]; lightning (Violet) and Andarna's fire reportedly kill them (disputed) [ER].
- Known: Xaden (turned in Onyx Storm, at least Asim, possibly Sage), Jack Barlowe, Theophanie, Brewyn [BG]. Silver-haired venin villain [WP].

## 5. Wards and wardstones
- Wards: circular protective fields projected from large rune-carved pillars (wardstones); powered by dragonfire of all seven dens/species; they block all magic except the wards' own, so venin and gryphon fliers lose power inside [ER, BSR].
- Created by the First Six; Basgiath wardstone reactivated using fire from six dens plus Andarna (seventh) [WP Iron Flame]. Warrick's journal lied about the instructions; Lyra's holds the true ones, in Old Lucerish [WP, search].
- Cost: Lilith died powering the Basgiath stone; Jack destroyed it by sacrificing his dragon; Andarna powered Tyrrendor's, Leothan Aretia's; Sloane siphoned magic into a stone [BSR, WP]. Wards fail within months absent power; the "alloy" that powers them is finite [II, BG].
- Mira's signet extends wards; Nadine's unweaves them [ER].
- Gryphon fliers cannot wield within them.

## 6. Signets (complete list found)

### 6.1 Mechanics
- Appear weeks or months after bonding; failure to manifest can kill the rider [AUD].
- Strength depends on dragon power and bond depth; reflects rider's personality or needs [AUD, II]. Colour does not determine signet type [AUD]. [II] claims it does; treat as unreliable.
- Lesser magic: open locks, move small objects, mage-light, speed, mental shields [II].
- Direct descendants of a dragon's prior rider (same family line) can gain a second signet or go mad; Rebellion Relic helps (every second-signet holder except Violet wears one) [LL snippet, AUD]. Xaden's second signet exists because Sgaeyl bonded his grandfather, which refutes the "all marked ones have two" theory [ER snippet].
- Mind shields block mind signets but also block your own [II].
- Cost: overusing causes burnout/death [II]; Violet's lightning drains her; Garrick's distance signet unknown cost.
- Rarity labels (from ER guides): Common, Rare, Exceptionally Rare, Unheard of, Unknown.

## 7. Intinnsic / forbidden magic
- Inntinnsic: signets that touch dream/mind realms; illegal in Navarre and holders are killed (Xaden hid his) [ER, WP]. The spelling is "Inntinnsic" in ER.
- Forbidden: drawing power from earth (venin), possession of Fables of the Barren (forbidden stories), bonding relatives of former riders (dragon law).
- Alloy: finite metal powering the wards; also kills venin and, in dagger form, dragons [BSR, BG].

## 8. Relics
- Rebellion relics: marks on 107 children, see glossary. Also "dragon relic" in II meaning the conduit; a piece of alloy in a conduit can let a rider channel venin power [BSR].

## 10. Game-oriented summary
- Dragon model families: 6 tail variants x 7 colours; tail independent; sizes: feathertail small, adult about 25 ft.
- Mind-link UI: bond bar, mate status, distance.
- Signet system: per-rider unique, scales with dragon power; treat a few starter signets (lightning, fire, ice, air, shadow) as data in `src/data/`.
- Wards: zones where magic is nullified except for wards; gryphon fliers nerfed inside.
