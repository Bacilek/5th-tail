## 2. Conscription Day and the road to being a rider

- **Conscription Day**: every year on 15 July (?) twenty-year-olds across Navarre report to Basgiath and choose a quadrant: Riders, Scribes, Healers, Infantry [ER]. Another wiki gives "mid to late July" (conflict) [FD snippet]. The Riders Quadrant accepts no conscripts, only volunteers/those who choose it [ER].
- **The Parapet**: a narrow stone walkway about two hundred feet up and eighteen inches wide (reading guide, via search) over a canyon; candidates must cross to enter the Riders Quadrant. Estimates of deaths: around 15 percent [ER Riders Quadrant], 15 to 20 percent [ER Conscription Day]. Game: wind, falling, other candidates pushing; good first-level challenge.
- **Entering**: after crossing and entering the edifice they become cadets [ER].
- **Year one**: build combat and academic skills for the **Gauntlet** [ER].
- **The Gauntlet**: a vertical obstacle course built into the ridgeline between citadel and flight field, about two months after entry, early autumn. Those who fail die [ER, search snippet]. Professor Emetterio oversees it [WP].
- **Presentation**: survivors of the Gauntlet are presented to the dragons; follows directly [ER].
- **Threshing**: two days after Presentation, in the valley beneath the citadel. Dragons choose; they may bond, ignore, or kill a candidate [ER]. Date: 1 October per one wiki (?) [FD snippet]. Bonded cadets become riders: get rider leathers and a private room [ER]. Unbonded survivors may repeat the year (?) [FD snippet].
- **Bonding mark**: a mark appears on the skin after bonding [ink and imaginings dragon guide, II].
- **Numbers**: about 100 dragons willing to bond in Violet's year, down from 137 and 163 in the prior two years [II dragons guide]. About a quarter of cadets graduate; most riders die before retirement [ER].
- **War Games**: harsh multi-wing competition in the second half of the year; riders from all wings; in Fourth Wing the final round went to Athebyne and was a trap [ER, WP].
- **Battle Brief**: strategy/tactics class taught by Professors Devera and Markham (WP) or Devera and Kiandra (II) (conflict on the second name). Colonel Markham is also described as head of the Scribes Quadrant (II) vs. Battle Brief professor (WP): conflict; a book reader should settle it.
- **Other classes** [WP, II]: Wielding (Prof Carr), Dragonkind (Prof Kaori), Signets (Prof Carr, fire wielder, dragon Breugan), sparring/combat (Prof Emetterio), Riders Safety Course (Prof Grady), Rider Survival Course (teaches surviving behind enemy lines without your dragon) [ER], Aretian Runes (Trissa, at Aretia).
- **Graduation**: 10 July, ten days after Reunification Day (1 July). New lieutenants get missives with assignments and leave; described as anticlimactic [ER].

## 3. Structure of the Riders Quadrant

- Four wings, each with three sections named Flame, Claw, Tail; each section has three squads of about 15 to 16 cadets [ER]. (So about 36 squads and ~570 cadets, but Courtyard holds up to 1000.)
- Cadet posts: **wingleader** and **section leader** are the prestige posts, usually third-years, sometimes exceptional second-years; **squad leader** below [ER]. Named: Ewan Faber, Fourth Wing wingleader; Iris Drue, First Wing; Aura Beinhaven, Second Wing (fire) [II]. Dain Aetos was a third-year wingleader [II]. Violet's squad is Second Squad "Iron Squad" (Fourth Wing, mixed years) [II].
- Wing numbers: First to Fourth; Xaden led Fourth Wing in Fourth Wing book [ER Tyrrish Rebellion page].
- Squad identity: they have squadmates' names and informal sub-roles (second-in-command, e.g. Liam Mairi) [II].

## 4. Ranks and titles

Confirmed in sources:
- Cadet (1st to 3rd year), Wingleader, Section Leader, Squad Leader [ER].
- **Lieutenant**: entry rank for graduates [ER]; Xaden is called a second lieutenant [II]; Brennan is Lieutenant Colonel [II].
- **Captain**: Mira Sorrengail [II, WP]; Captain Drake Cordella and Captain Lera Dorrell appear as authors of compendium/guide texts [ER venin page].
- **Major**: Major Afendra, author of the "unofficial guide" excerpts used as chapter epigraphs [ER].
- **Lieutenant Colonel**: Dengrensi (Samara), Brennan [II].
- **Colonel**: Aetos, Kaori, Markham (WP); Panchek is Commandant of the Riders Quadrant (Colonel, II); Felix Gerault is Colonel, Aretian Assembly [II]; Colonel Mairi (rebel side) [ER].
- **Commanding General**: Lilith Sorrengail leads the college (WP). **General**: Augustine Melgren commands all armies (WP); II lists Melgren as "General". There is a conflict on who is the top: Lilith (college) vs Melgren (all armies). Fine as two separate offices.
- **Commandant**: head of the Riders Quadrant (Panchek) [II].
- Titles of nobility outrank military rank in Navarre: a duke/duchess outranks riders [BSR / ER ending-explained]. Duke Lindell (Tirivainne), Duke Lewellen [II]. Prince Halden Tauri; the nobles' assembly is the **Senarium** [BSR, II].
- Poromish: Viscount Tecarus, Queen Maraya (queen killed by venin per BSR).
- Gryphon fliers: **flier** (not rider). Alias spelling "flyer" on ER.
- Venin ranks (in creatures file).

Not found online: formal insignia, exact pay grades, who is "major general". (?)

## 5. Uniforms and colours

- Riders wear **black leathers**, designed to hide blood and confuse enemies; summer and lined winter versions; riders get their own fitted leathers after bonding [ER Riders Quadrant].
- **Fliers** (gryphon riders) wear **brown leathers** [ER gryphons page].
- Venin wear robes (often purple per ER venin page, though BG says red), carry staves and red fighting leathers [ER, BG] (conflict on colour; both could be true: robes purple, fighting gear red).
- Wing/section colours: **none found**. Section names (Flame, Claw, Tail) are the identifiers; no per-wing colour in any source. A book reader should confirm whether wings have colour tabs. (?) Design option for the game: invent subtle colour tabs and mark them "invented".
- Scribes, healers, infantry clothing: not found online. From general memory scribes wear plain robes and healers wear a distinct colour (?). Mark as not verified.
- Gathering Hall pillars are carved as dragons in colours red, green, orange, blue, black [ER Riders Quadrant] (brown and irid not included; interesting visual cue).
- Marked ones: **rebellion relics**, swirling silvery-looking scars on the arm (?) of children whose parents were executed by dragonfire; 107 bearers; Xaden (24) oldest [ER]. Mechanically the mark came from a rune made by Colonel Mairi, Liam Mairi (?) and Sloane's mother, folded into stones given to officers' children; it activates when a parent dies by dragonfire. Intended to shield against the executing dragon-rider's signet [ER]. Earlier belief: mark deliberately made by Codagh (WP, II). The ER page itself says it will be updated: conflict. Relic-bearers can hold a second signet only when the dragon previously bonded an ancestor; the relic helps survive that [LL snippet, AUD].
- Dragon "relic" in II means the conduit through which dragons channel magic to riders: a separate use of the word.

## 6. History and politics

- **Navarre founding / First Six**: Navarre's wards were created by the First Six (first bonded riders) at what is now Basgiath [ER dragons page, WP Iron Flame]. After the Great War the provinces united for the first time, protected by wards and first bonded riders [ER Great War]. Year numbering "AU" (after unification) is used; first gryphon attack in 1 AU [ER gryphons]. Rebellion 627 years after Tyrrendor joined; Battle of Aretia dated 1 July 628 AU (?) and Treaty of Aretia 635 AU (?) per fandom snippet; only that wiki states dates.
- **Great War**: about 600+ years ago; General Daramor's army nearly destroyed the continent; allies went home; dragons claimed the west, gryphons the centre; Barrens abandoned [ER]. Brennan suggests something killed the venin then, and the characters seek that weapon [ER].
- **Tyrrish Rebellion** (Tyrrendor calls it apostasy; Navarre calls it the secession): led by Fen Riorson (Xaden's father), crushed, leaders executed publicly with children forced to watch [ER]. General Lilith Sorrengail led the suppression [WP]. Rebel children sent to the Riders Quadrant as hostages/wards, called "marked ones". Fen was executed by Lilith (WP: "Lilith carried out the execution"; elsewhere dragon Codagh executed parents; conflict on who).
- **Reunification Day**: 1 July, royal proclamation by King Tauri commemorating the Battle of Aretia and the Treaty of Aretia; honours the dead of the war against the separatists [FD snippet, ER]. Likely feast/parade day; game: festival banners.
- **Second rebellion**: covertly led by Xaden's group; Aretian Assembly (seven people, per II "Assembly of Aretia") as informal leadership; goal is exposing the truth about venin and what the ward stones cost [II].
- **Cover-up**: Navarre's wards run on a specific alloy; leaders chose wards over anti-venin weapons; a fabricated war with Poromiel hides the truth; Poromiel raids are alloy raids; new officers learn the truth only at high rank [BG, fan analysis, treat as theory partially].
- **Navarre-Poromiel trade**: over 200 years, quarterly exchange: Navarre sends meat and lumber, Poromiel cloth and farm goods [search snippet, unclear source].
- **Pardons**: after Iron Flame riders who deserted to Aretia were pardoned by a royal decree; Xaden made Duke of Tyrrendor [BSR, search].
- **Marriage**: requires a priestess and a temple; Xaden and Violet wed at Dunne's temple outside Aretia [ER]. (Violet's memory of it was erased by Imogen at the end of Onyx Storm.)
- **Funerals**: the dead are burned [BSR].

## 8. Technology, weapons, economy

- Tech level: pre-gunpowder, medieval: stone fortresses, leather, steel, runes, magic-augmented; written ledgers, quills/"ink pens" as lesser magic [II dragons guide]; ships exist (Onyx Storm sails to isles) [WP]. Mage-lights as lesser magic.
- Weapons: daggers are central (Violet carries many on her leathers); **alloy-hilted daggers** kill venin but weaken after a few strikes [BG]; swords; bows (?) ; staves for sparring (?); Violet's armoury of dagger sheaths in boots/forearm is from memory (?). Smuggled daggers armed gryphon fliers vs venin [WP Iron Flame snippet].
- Sparring ring at Basgiath, the "rider's ring": where riders are made or broken (epigraph) [ER].
- Lesser magic available to all bonded riders: open/lock doors, move objects, speed, mage-lights, runes, mental shields [II, II dragons guide].
- Wardstones: pillars with runes (see creatures file).
- Currency: **no currency named** in any source I found; only a gem as the price of an audience on Deverelli [BSR]. Books likely mention coin (?) Unverified.
- Food and drink: no verified specifics online; Talia's husband poisoned dinner at Hedotis [BSR]. From memory the cadets eat in the dining hall, tea and bread appear; unverified (?).
- Medicine: Healers Quadrant trains healers; Brennan's mender signet (exceptionally rare [ER guide]); Nolon also mender; Violet's joints (Ehlers-Danlos-like, hypermobility in the book; (?) not sourced here). Dyre is a second-year healer [II]. Signet-blocking serum being tested on Jack Barlowe [BG].
- Law: treason for questioning leadership (loyalist view) [II]; Code of Conduct governs signet teaching [BSR]; using inntinnsic signet is illegal (II/BSR); dragons are forbidden from bonding relatives of previous riders (AUD) but happens.

## 10. Visual/gameplay hooks (summary)

- Hub: Basgiath cliff fortress, black-leather cadets, Gathering Hall dragon pillars in five colours, Courtyard (teardrop, 10 ft thick outer wall).
- Parapet crossing = tutorial balance challenge. Gauntlet = vertical obstacle run. Threshing valley = dragon choice scene. War Games = squad vs squad.
- Rank ribbons: invented; none canonical.
- Flier brown leathers vs rider black leathers; venin purple robes and red leathers.
- Marked ones: arm scar glow, 107 total.
