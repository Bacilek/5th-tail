# Lore DB: Cadet Characters (Basgiath Riders Quadrant and allies)

Scope: first/second/third-year cadets and close student-level allies. Sources are fan/reference sites; no book text was available, so everything is paraphrased from them.
Labels: [FW] Fourth Wing, [IF] Iron Flame, [OS] Onyx Storm. SPOILERS throughout.
Source tags: ER = empyreanriders.com, BG = bookishgoblin.com, SN = sparknotes, SS = supersummary, BC = bookcompanion.com, EW = Empyrean fan wiki (only via search snippets, page itself blocked), AUD = Audible blog.
(?) = uncertain or conflicting. "(memory)" = from general knowledge of the series, not verified on any fetched page; treat as lowest confidence.
Ignore shapes.inc "avatar" pages; they are AI chatbots and contradict canon.

Global notes useful for modelling
- Rider uniform: black leathers. Dragon relic (a scar-like mark from the bond) sits on the back/torso; a rebellion relic (a "marked" child of the Tyrrish rebellion) is a dark mark spiralling along an arm. Size of the relic reflects the person's standing in the rebellion (Xaden biggest, Garrick second biggest). Patches on uniforms show weapon skill (ER Garrick).
- Quadrant structure: wings (First to Fourth) > sections (Flame, Tail, Claw, Wing, ...) > squads. Fourth Wing is Xaden's wing. Sections and squad names appear below where sources agree.
- Dragon tail types matter for the game: Daggertail, Swordtail, Clubtail, Scorpiontail, Morningstar, Feathertail.

## Violet Sorrengail
- Role: protagonist. Daughter of General Lilith Sorrengail. Trained as a Scribe, forced into the Riders Quadrant. First-year in [FW], second-year in [IF], third-year in [OS] (about 21 at [IF] start per ER).
- Wing/section/squad: Fourth Wing, Flame Section, Second Squad (Iron Squad) in [FW]/[IF]; Squad structure changes later. (ER/EW; Iron Squad name per fan wiki.)
- LOOKS (well sourced, ER):
  - Petite: roughly 5'2"-5'3", under 115 lb at the start. Delicate face, fair/pale skin.
  - Light hazel eyes that read blue or amber depending on light; more green in later books.
  - Long brown hair fading to steel-silver at the ends (colour pattern persists after cutting). Combat: crown braid so nobody can grab it.
  - Joint problems (Ehlers-Danlos-like): wraps and metal clasps on dislocation-prone joints; small boot size.
  - Scar on her abdomen (about 3 in) from a venin blade, [IF] onward. Dragon relic on her back (shown through gown backs).
  - Kit: black riding leathers, dragonscale corset-style armour with hidden dagger sheaths, many daggers; formal silk gown cut low at the back for the relic.
- Dragons: Tairn (ancient black Morningstar, Naolin's former dragon, mated to Sgaeyl). Andarna (small golden Feathertail in [FW]; listed as an "irid" Scorpiontail with iridescent look in [OS] guide, so her appearance changes as she matures).
- Signets: Lightning wielding (via Tairn; appears at War Games in anger). Time-stopping is a gift from Andarna, temporary, lost after Resson (ER says lost). Dream walking (via Andarna), [IF] hints, [OS] full.
- Personality / voice: scholar first; sharp, dry, self-deprecating inner voice; stubborn; compassionate but willing to kill; talks back to dragons and authority. Protective of friends, chronic self-doubt early. Mannerisms: touching her braid, checking dagger placement, reading everything.
- Relationships: Xaden (love, mate/eventual husband [OS]); Dain (childhood friend turned estranged); Rhiannon (best friend); Ridoc/Sawyer/Jesinia; Imogen (mentor to ally); Tairn/Andarna; mother Lilith; brothers Brennan (thought dead, alive in [FW] end) and sister Mira.
- Arc: [FW] survives Parapet/Gauntlet/Threshing, bonds two dragons, War Games, Resson battle where Liam dies, ends wounded. [IF] assassination attempts, torture by Varrish (she nearly breaks), finds out truths about the wards, final battle at Basgiath where Lilith dies; Xaden becomes venin. [OS] grief and secrecy about Xaden's venin state, Andarna leaves then returns, dream walking, Xaden marries her (ER; marriage exact form (?)).
- Memorable places: Parapet, Gauntlet, Threshing field, Riorson House, Scribe Quadrant library.
- Game: she is the player/anchor. Show crown braid, silver tips, wraps, dagger sheaths; low-stamina feel, low health pool; lightning as a late-game spell.

## Xaden Riorson
- Role: Wingleader of Fourth Wing in [FW] (age about 23), later Duke of Tyrrendor (title confirmed in [OS] guide). Fen Riorson's son. Shadow wielder; Inntinnsic revealed [IF].
- LOOKS (well sourced, ER/AUD):
  - Very tall (over 6'4" per ER; "well over six feet" AUD), broad and muscular.
  - Black tousled hair, dark brows, strong jaw, dark stubble. Tawny/brown skin.
  - Onyx eyes with gold flecks (AUD says they shift toward amber/rimmed red after venin; [OS] permanent red veins/rim around eyes).
  - Diagonal scar through left eyebrow ending at upper cheek (AUD says it came from Sgaeyl; ER does not say).
  - Rebellion relic from left wrist up arm to neck/jaw. Back covered by Sgaeyl's relic plus 107 scars, one per child of the rebellion he answers for.
  - Smell: mint, leather, a citrus-floral note (ER).
- Dragon: Sgaeyl (blue Daggertail, around 50 years old, once bonded to his grandfather). Linked to Tairn as mates.
- Signet: Shadow wielding (shadows suppress and subdue). Inntinnsic [IF]. Venin asim [OS].
- Personality: controlled, secretive, sharp-tongued; commanding public speaker (like his father); trusts only a very few (Garrick, Bodhi, Liam); disdains the Codex. Mannerisms: stillness, silence as threat, half-smirk, eyes soften at Violet.
- Arc: [FW] hostile mentor, falls for Violet, sent to Athebyne after Dain's report, returns; confesses love. [IF] Inntinnsic reveal; rebels; at the end he reaches into the ground to save Violet and turns venin (ER wording). [OS] duke, hides condition, red veins, loses control at times.
- Game: boss-ranked ally. Needs shadow VFX, relic mesh, gold-flecked eye texture.

## Dain Aetos
- Role: Violet's childhood friend; second-year squad leader [FW]; Fourth Wing Wingleader [IF] (replacing Xaden); [OS] Fourth Wing leadership and Sloane's partner.
- LOOKS (ER Dain page, fairly detailed): tall, broad, strong fighter's build; tan skin; sandy-brown eyes; close-cropped light-brown curls; neatly trimmed beard over a chin scar; calloused hands; black leathers with a dagger tucked discreetly; a red dragon tattoo on his shoulder hidden by short sleeves; smells of leather and soap. Used to grin constantly; now usually stern. (Conflict (?): other guide says soft brown eyes; hair sandy-brown vs light-brown curls; ER Fourth Wing guide says full lips.)
- Dragon: Cath (red Swordtail). Signet: retrocognition (read recent memories through touch, often face; keyword search).
- Personality: rule-bound Codex follower, controlling-protective, loyal, rigid but conflicted. Voice: measured, parental-sounding ("for your own good"); softer with Sloane later.
- Relationships: Violet (childhood bond, kiss after Threshing then retreat); Xaden (mutual distrust); Aaric (childhood friend, had him moved to Fourth Wing); Amber Mavis (close as cadets); Colonel Aetos (father; turns against him [IF]); Varrish (former mentor, later killed by Dain); Sloane (guilt over Liam, romance [OS]).
- Arc: [FW] alienates Violet; reports to father; sided against her after Amber's attack. [IF] guilt, redemption, kills Varrish, joins the revolution, leaves for Aretia. [OS] steals from his father to help Violet; falls for Sloane.
- Game: NPC squad leader, shifts from antagonist to ally.

## Rhiannon Matthias
- Role: Violet's best friend; first-year [FW]; second-year Squad Leader [IF]; leader in [OS]. Hails from Morraine Province, twin sister Raegan, nephew Lukas.
- LOOKS (ER): dark skin, high cheekbones, oval face, brown eyes, dark brown hair in several rows of short braids; US shoe size 8. Height not given; a snippet implies she is taller than Calvin. Uniform quirks unknown.
- Dragon: Feirge (green Daggertail). Signet: Summoner (calls objects or beings to her). ER Fourth Wing guide says rare. (SS snippet says "teleport objects" in [OS] summary. (?))
- Personality: loyal, brave, principled, patient. Voice: steady, calm authority, kind teasing; stands up to rank.
- Relationships: Violet (anchor); Ridoc (banter, deepening); Tara (romance, fling); Sawyer (clashes, fling; made him XO); Sloane (closer in [OS]).
- Arc: [FW] welcomes Violet, trains her in exchange for history lessons, promises to wait on the parapet. [IF] becomes squad leader, rebel advocate. [OS] steady voice, rallies riders, begins her own path.
- Game: first companion; healer/support archetype alternative with summon spell.

## Ridoc Gamlyn
- Role: class-clown friend; first-year [FW]; Iron Squad.
- LOOKS (ER): shorter cadet; dark brown hair; brown skin; tattoo of a brown swordtail on his neck added in [IF] (Aotrom's relic art). Height conflict (?): EW snippet says half a foot taller than Tynan and twice as wide, which suggests short but stocky/broad.
- Dragon: Aotrom (brown Swordtail, 22 years old per ER). Signet: ice wielding (common).
- Personality: sarcastic, witty, rarely loses his temper, uses jokes to defuse; fiercely loyal. [OS] angry when Violet hid Xaden's condition.
- Relationships: Violet, Rhiannon, Sawyer, Jesinia (friends); Tyvon Varen (fling).
- Arc: [FW] funny ice wielder; [IF] more serious; [OS] strategy and morale. Fan theory of a second dragon (ER) is unconfirmed.

## Imogen Cardulo
- Role: Fourth Wing, third-year rider in [IF]; part of Xaden's inner circle. [FW] intimidating mentor; [IF]/[OS] ally.
- LOOKS (ER, repeated by Audible/AUD): half-shaved, half-pink hair; a head taller than Violet; toned build; pale green eyes; hoop piercings along both ears; rebellion relic coiling around her forearm. (Reader comment says the ear piercings belong to Quinn (?).)
- Dragon: Glane (orange Daggertail). Signets: memory erasure (wipes recent memory); second, rock wielding, in [OS].
- Personality: blunt, pragmatic, survivalist, then protective leader; softer with Sloane. Voice: short, flat, sarcastic. Mannerism: hard stares, mat-sparring.
- Relationships: Quinn (best friend, dies in [OS]); Garrick (slow attraction); Sloane; Violet; Xaden.
- Arc: see above; grief for Quinn in [OS].
- Conflict (?): BC says "tries to kill Violet" (a match in [FW]); sites differ on the exact event.

## Garrick Tavis
- Role: Xaden's oldest friend; third-year rider and Section Leader in [FW]; Flame Section leader (BC/BG); Xaden's second.
- LOOKS (ER): dark-haired, pale (?), wide shoulders, dense muscle; hazel eyes; second-largest rebellion relic; weapon-skill patches on the uniform. Height conflicting ("tall" and "short") (?). BG says brown hair; ER Fourth Wing guide says dark hair.
- Dragon: Chradh (brown Scorpiontail). Signets: air wielding and distance wielding.
- Personality: calm, mission first, steady contrast to Imogen/Violet, quietly confident, trains younger riders. Voice: terse and polite-bland. Held hostage in [OS] and stays calm.
- Relationships: Xaden, Bodhi, Imogen (hinted), Violet. Father was Fen's right hand.

## Sawyer Henrick
- Role: repeating first-year [FW] (failed Threshing once, age 20-21); Executive Officer of Iron Squad [IF]; metallurgist.
- LOOKS (ER/Audible via ER): tall, lean, wiry; fair; freckles along the jaw; brown stubble; brown hair (BG); [IF] loses lower leg (he builds his own prosthetic in [OS]).
- Dragon: Sliseag (red Swordtail). Signet: metallurgy.
- Personality: calm, level-headed, intelligent, natural leader of first-years, doesn't retaliate. Learns sign language for Jesinia.
- Relationships: Violet, Ridoc, Rhiannon, Jesinia (love interest).
- Arc: [FW] hides repeat from family; Violet trades history for combat help; [IF] loses leg at Basgiath; [OS] rehab, flies again.
- Game: could show visible prosthetic variant.

## Liam Mairi (dies in [FW])

- LOOKS (ER): tall; spiky blond hair; strong features; prominent nose; sky-blue eyes; dimple when smiling.
- Dragon: Deigh (red Daggertail). Signets: farsightedness; ice wielding revealed at Resson.
