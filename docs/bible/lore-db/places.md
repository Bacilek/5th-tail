## 1. GEOGRAPHY & TRAVEL (overview)

### Continent layout
- Navarre = western half of the Continent; Poromiel = east of Navarre; the Barrens = east of Poromiel (BF). The Esben Mountains form the natural frontier between Navarre and Poromiel (BF).
- Magical wards from the Vale form a roughly circular protective shell over Navarre; beyond it are unwarded outposts that only the military holds (BF).
- Navarre: six provinces (BF names Morraine, Tyrrendor, Elsum; also Calldyr, Luceras, Deaconshire/Deconshire as domains; exact province list (?)).
  - Morraine: Basgiath War College, the Vale; rivers Iakobos (and Beatha) are fertile valleys (BF).
  - Tyrrendor: southernmost and largest province (ER); Aretia, Fortress of Aretia, Cliffs of Dralor (south/east frontier).
  - Elsum: Samara; borders Krovla (BF).
  - Calldyr: royal seat, west, borders Luceras and Tyrrendor (ER).
  - Luceras: home province of Warrick (one of the First Six) (BF).
- Poromiel: three provinces (BF): Krovla (south; Cordyn, Draithus, Resson, Pavis, Soudra), Braevick (central; Zolya, Anca, Cliffsbane), Cygnisen (north, culturally independent, "Cygni"). Terrain: arid grasslands, fertile plains, marshes; products: grain, textiles, crystalline gems that amplify minor magic (BF).
- The Cliffs of Dralor: wall of 12,000+ ft cliffs running from the rough Arctile Ocean down to the lowest plains of the Tyrrendor plateau; Medaro Pass is the only way through from the Poromiel side (ER, BF). They separate Aretia/Tyrrendor from Poromiel.
- Isle Kingdoms: five large + thirteen smaller islands ringing the Continent (ER). The big ones named: Deverelli, Unnbriel, Hedotis, Zehyllna, Loysam. A second sea (Cerlian Sea) holds dozens of minor isles [OS] (ER recap). No official detailed isle map exists; position of isles is fan reconstruction (BF map).

## 2. BASGIATH WAR COLLEGE

### Overall
- Built into the side of Basgiath Mountain, Navarre (Morraine Province). Large fortress with tall, several-storey stone battlements; defensive turrets at each corner, one holds the bell (ER). BF: southern turret = bell tower beside a worn cobblestone path; northern turret links to Infantry at ground level; four roads climb the grassy fields to the main gate; each quadrant has its own entrance (?). BF also says the lower fortress holds Scribe/Healer/Infantry/Admin and the Riders Citadel is above. "Basgiath" is also given as Navarre's capital-city name by ER (?; Calldyr is the king's seat).
- Four quadrants: Riders, Infantry, Healers, Scribes (ER). Uniform/colour cues (BF): Riders black leathers, Healers pale blue (?), Scribes cream hooded tunics, Infantry navy (?). ER confirms Scribes wear cream.
- Named indoor/outdoor places on ER page: Administration Building, General Sorrengail's Office, Bell Tower, First Years' area, Dorms, Gathering Hall, Academic Wing, Dragon Rotunda, Parapet, Torture Chamber, Gauntlet, Flight Field.
- Mood: stone, marble, cobblestone, mage-lit lamps, fog, wind, drop into chasm (BF). Mid-July Conscription Day; deadly culture.
- Game idea: one hub mountain level with vertical zones: Parapet entrance tower -> Citadel -> Gauntlet cliff -> Flight Field -> Vale below; fast-travel bell tower.

### Administration Building / General Sorrengail's Office / Bell Tower
- Only named by ER; no description. Administration houses the Commandant (General Sorrengail, Violet's mother) office [FW]. Bell announces formations/emergencies (?). Game idea: classic Skyrim "main hall" with large map table, wards schematic.

### Riders Quadrant (the Citadel)
- Separate citadel on its own, housed apart from the main building and reached by the Parapet (ER). Only Riders enter through it.
- Dragon Rotunda: three-storey hall, marble floor, glass dome; two large arched doors on one side lead to the Academic Wing, two on the other lead to the dorms; six steps up to four doorways into the Gathering Hall (ER). BF: six marble dragon statues (?).
- Courtyard: large teardrop-shaped, holds 1,000 riders; the rounded end is an outer wall at least ten feet thick; stone halls line the sides (ER).
- Academic Wing: four storeys carved into the mountain with a curved end (ER). Contains classrooms, sparring gym, Burn Pit (BF, ?). Library with every book a rider needs (ER).
- Gathering Hall: six marble pillars carved as dragons, coloured red, green, orange, blue, black (each = a dragon type); central floor big enough for at least four squads (ER). Used for assemblies, dining (?), squad lists. (Count of 6 pillars vs 5 listed colours: ER inconsistency (?).)
- Dorms: built high on the cliff (ER). Room contents (beds, desks, shared bath) (?) - book reader needed.
- The Forge heated by dragonfire (BF, ?).
- Scenes: Rider candidates assemble in the courtyard after the Parapet [FW]; Gathering Hall used for announcements and squad assignments [FW]; Dragon Rotunda is the crossroads between academics and dorms [FW].

### Parapet
- Stone bridge connecting the main building (Healer, Scribe, Infantry quadrants) to the Riders Quadrant (ER).
- Width about 18 inches, height roughly 200 feet above the ground (ER, BF, readers' highlights).
- Surface: unevenly shaped stones set in mortar (ER). Fandom snippet: slick-looking but not mossy-slippery; the entrance sits ~12 inches above a gravel courtyard (?).
- Approach: candidates climb a tower - a dim curved staircase, ~250 steps, equidistant windows, no railing (Fandom snippet, BF) - and emerge at a circular turret-top with stone crenellations and a gap that is the entrance (Fandom snippet). Strong wind at that height can blow people off; fog and wind make it worse (BF). No railing on the span (BF).
- Casualties: roughly 15% of candidates die each Conscription Day (ER; BF says ~15-20% on the main page) (?).
- Scene: Conscription Day, mid-July: Violet crosses with Dain/Rhiannon/Xaden-adjacent cohort; candidates fall to a ravine below; Riders Quadrant gate at the far side [FW]. Details of the ravine floor (rocks? river?) (?).
- Game idea: long, tense balance-crossing mini-level with wind gusts, fog bands and other NPC candidates falling; camera cinematic of drop.

### Gauntlet
- Vertical obstacle course carved into a ridgeline so steep it passes for a cliff; guards the Vale; separates the citadel from the flight field. Must be finished within two months of joining Riders Quadrant or the cadet is killed/removed; success leads to Presentation (ER). Name comes from Professor Emetterio's comparison to a metal gauntlet that's hard to grip, ~20 years ago (ER).
- Layout: zigzags upward in five switchbacks, each a 180-degree turn, each harder than the last; ends at the top of the bluff above the citadel/flight field/Vale (ER). BF: entered via narrow gate, mage-lit tunnel and a ten-foot stone arch (?), final ascent opens above the flight field; rough staircase carved in the cliff is the way up to the flight field (?).
- Obstacle list (BF/DS; ER only confirms last two as chimney + near-vertical ramp):
  1. First ascent: 15-foot rotating log; four granite pillars to climb between; a large turning wheel with one entrance (vertical, acts as switchback) (BF, DS).
  2. Second ascent: gigantic suspended iron spheres, slightly too big to grip; five in BF's count (BF, DS).
  3. Third ascent: three hanging textured metal cylinders/rods crossed by swinging arm over arm; then unstable shaking iron pillars to leap across (BF, DS).
  4. Fourth ascent: seven spinning timber logs about three feet in diameter, turning in alternating directions; then a vertical chimney, braced between two walls - too wide for small climbers (BF, DS, ER).
  5. Fifth ascent: a huge wooden ramp that juts out then curves back up, almost vertical, up to the cliff edge (ER, BF, DS, goodreads note). No ropes on the final run (BF).
- DS numbers 11 items (log, pillars, wheel, balls, switchback, cylinders, pillars, switchback, logs, chimney, ramp); BF groups them into five. They agree on content; (?) on gaps between.
- Rules: ropes allowed in practice; on Presentation Day (timed), a rope may only catch a fall, adding a 30-second penalty per use. Nine supervised practice sessions before the final run on September 29. Fastest squad presents first to dragons; slowest last, with poorer bonding odds. Helping another cadet is forbidden - penalties include disqualification/death (BF; ER confirms only the two-month and kill rule).
- Scene: Violet trains with Xaden's teaching and mind-over-muscle strategy, struggles with the chimney/ramp and overcomes via cleverness [FW]. Squad cheering from base; crowd at cliff edge (?).
- Game idea: signature climbing/parkour level; reuse as a timed mode with ropes penalties; moving logs and swinging spheres as physics set-pieces.

### Flight Field
- Secluded flat area above the Gauntlet; used for takeoff/landing and training; reached after the Gauntlet for Presentation (BF, fan snippets). BF calls it a box canyon (inference, not sourced). The Vale's mouth is at its narrowest point with a seasonal waterfall (Fandom snippet).
- Scenes: Presentation (cadets pick/appear before dragons), formation, flight training, Battle of Basgiath [FW, IF]. Actual dimensions (?).

### The Vale (hatching grounds, Threshing field)
- Sacred forested valley behind the college, home of the dragons, hatching grounds and Threshing field; anchors the wardstone and Navarre's wards (BF).
- Fandom snippet: bowl-shaped forested valley; greenery stretching south; squat trees and wildflower slopes; entrance at narrowest part of flight field with a seasonal waterfall; Threshing field lies southwest and beneath the citadel. BF: grassy expanse along the Iakobos River, hatching ground in open grass; scarred by the Battle of Basgiath.
- Aeries/dragon dens on cliffs (?); not described in readable sources.
- Scenes: Threshing (cadets walk toward dragons; most die) [FW]; Battle of Basgiath [FW, IF]; Andarna and the juvenile dragons [FW/IF].
- Game idea: ominous "walk through the field" Threshing level; day-night; dragon nests carved into cliffs.

### Infantry Quadrant
- Ground level, linked via the northern turret (BF). Barracks, open training fields, mess hall, amphitheatre (BF, ?). Navy uniforms (?). Little canon description found. Game idea: military training yard.

### Healers Quadrant
- Southern end of the college; trains healers and houses graduated healers and riders with the mender signet. Reached by a door at the bottom of a stairwell below main level, then a passage up a ravine (ER). BF: covered stone bridge over ravine (?), infirmary, apothecary labs, herb garden, rest wards; pale-blue uniforms (?).
- Healers swear an oath to never harm; menders follow only the rider code (ER).
- Scenes: injured cadets treated after Threshing/Gauntlet [FW, IF].
- Game idea: herb garden hub, healing quests.

### Scribes Quadrant and Archives
- Scribes Quadrant sits across a river from the other quadrants (ER); cream uniforms. BF places it underground via tunnels (?); conflict: "across a river" vs "underground tunnel" (?).
- Archives: cavernous, shelves about 20 feet tall running the building's length; smell of aged paper, binding glue and ink; scribes wear hoods to blend with books; copies/originals of nearly every book in Navarre except forbidden tomes (ER). BF adds transcription halls and a sealed Royal Vault (?).
- Scenes: Violet and Dain research; Violet reads secret histories; forbidden-book access [FW, IF].
- Game idea: stealth/puzzle library with forbidden section and hooded scribes.

### Torture Chamber
- Entrance hidden in the cliffside beneath the foundation walls of the Riders Quadrant; windowless, guarded cave (ER). Antechamber with a large wooden desk (one high-backed chair vs two chairs). Main room: dome, cobblestone walls, scarred wooden table, six chairs; five wooden beds without mattresses; two steel-braced doors with barred windows and steel latches; door to attached bathroom; not sound-shielded (ER). Introduced in the Rider Survival Course.
- Scenes: interrogations/prisoners [FW, IF]. Game idea: dark short dungeon.

### Other Basgiath items
- Burn Pit (BF), sparring gym/mat room (BF, ?), mess/dining (?), infirmary, Battle Brief room [OS] (ER recap), "the pit" - a sparring arena with sound shield and outer cobblestone rings [OS] (ER recap).
- Chantara: village supplying Basgiath; second-years of Healer, Scribe and Infantry allowed, Riders barred for nearly a decade after a brawl burned down a pub (ER). No visual description found (?). Game idea: tavern village (the burned pub could be a rebuilt ruin).

## 3. TYRRENDOR AND ARETIA

### Aretia (town)
- Capital of Tyrrendor; site of the Battle of Aretia (commemorated July 1 as Reunification Day) (ER). In a valley ringed by hills and caves (BF; "eastern Tyrrendor" (?)).
- Navarrese forces burned it after the Tyrrish Rebellion; ruins for decades; rebuilt with green-roofed houses, a forge, printing press, facilities for riders, dragons and gryphon fliers (BF; ER confirms uniform green roofs).
- Thermal valley: warm year-round, snow doesn't settle (BF). Hatching grounds in the valley above Aretia - ancestral place of the Dubhmadinn black dragons (BF, ER); active again [IF]; also training, rune-weaving, drills.
- Temple of Amari with columns carved in Tyrrish runes (BF, ?).
- Wardstone: trail behind Riorson House climbs a ridge and narrows to a canyon, opening into a circular chamber hidden from above by masking runes; a black pillar twice Xaden's height, wide enough for nine to surround, carved with concentric circles and runes; cold when inactive; Leothan breathes on it to activate wards [IF] (BF; details (?)).
- Travel: ~18 h dragon from Basgiath; a few hours from Cliffs of Dralor.

### Fortress of Aretia / Riorson House
- Seat of the Assembly and home of Xaden in Tyrrendor (ER). Built into a defensible mountainside near the Cliffs of Dralor; blends palace elegance with military strength; thick stone survived dragon fire; never breached by an army across three sieges (ER).
- Exterior: marble steps down to a gravel courtyard; spiral stair to a roof turret overlooking the town; steel-reinforced double doors, cobblestone entry (BF, ER).
- Interior: tall arched foyer with patterned carpet and twin polished staircases climbing ~4 floors, morning light through small windows high on the entrance wall; Poromish tapestry across the foyer; real artworks (ER). Great Hall with trestle tables; Assembly Chamber (two-storey, long table on a dais, seats ~30; throne of half polished walnut, half blackened wood topped by a carved sleeping dragon); rebuilt theater in the northwest wing with a map of the Known World including the Isles; two-storey library with only first floor stocked; sparring gym with nine mats; family quarters on second floor (Violet and Xaden's room with big bed, bookshelves, bathing chamber, blanket from Xaden's mother); barracks on upper three floors (BF, ER; room-level detail (?)).
- Scenes: Violet healed by Brennan at Aretia after poisoned dagger [FW]; Assembly meetings, war council [IF, OS]; Xaden's throne [IF/OS].
- Game idea: second hub; ward ritual puzzle at the wardstone; hatching-ground cave.

### Cliffs of Dralor
- 12,000+ ft rock wall between Arctile Ocean and Tyrrendor plateau; Medaro Pass the sole pass (ER). Protects Tyrrendor's eastern/southern frontier (BF). Scenes: Draithus is called last stronghold before them (ER); Medaro Pass refugee evacuation [OS]. Game idea: sheer mountain flight corridor with updrafts.

## 4. SOUTHERN WING OUTPOSTS

### Athebyne
- Border fortress in southeast Navarre; near Tyrrendor and the town Resson; across the border a small Poromish village of ~300 (ER). Square fortress, four towers; walls thin enough that a dragon can take off from the top; same design as Montserrat (ER). Commanded by Major Kallista Neema, XO of the Southern Wing (ER). Supply and battle hub; trade between Poromiel and Navarre happens here four times a year (ER).
- Scenes: Xaden and the marked ones hand out weapons to Poromish fliers [FW]; Battle of Resson nearby - Violet stabbed with poisoned dagger, Liam Mairi dies [FW]; Dain/Aetos sent beyond wards to set up war games HQ [FW] (ER snippet, ?).

### Montserrat
- Military outpost, Morraine Province near the Cygnisen border (ER). Commander Major Quade (ER). Weather: warmer than Basgiath (ER). Same square four-tower plan. Squad tour week [FW] (?). Sipene village about an hour away by air.

### Samara
- Easternmost outpost of the Southern Wing, Elsum Province, 1-2 miles from the Poromish border, about a day from Basgiath (ER, BF).
- Very large fortress of dark red stone, about twice the size of Athebyne/Montserrat; stone ramp from trampled grass to an arched entrance with spiked portcullis and two sentries; a cage four stories up outside the entrance sometimes holds infantry (?); wide stone hallways; barracks on the south side; infirmary in the bailey; fighting pit dug into the foundation with bloody dirt floor and iron rungs; mage-lit sparring gym (ER, BF).
- Xaden's room: third floor south wing, second door on the right; windows on one side look onto the bailey, wooden doors on the other; sparse sturdy furniture, heavy black curtains, huge bed with thick dark-grey blanket (ER). Esben peaks snow-capped in summer.
- Scenes: Xaden and Garrick Tavis stationed here [IF]; Varrish's earlier posting (letter about three prisoners dying) (ER). Game idea: fortress level with pit fights, remote harsh mountain base.

### Sipene
- High-altitude village in the Esben Mountains, on the Navarre/Braevick border, outside the wards, ~1 h flight from Montserrat (ER). No description (?). Game idea: snow village raid.

### Resson
- Border trading post between Krovla (Poromiel) and Tyrrendor/Elsum (ER/BF conflict), on plains at the foot of the Esben Mountains, short flight from Athebyne; ~300 residents; clock tower is the only landmark (ER); mines used as shelter (BF).
- Battle of Resson: July 2, 634 AU - venin and wyverns attack; riders and fliers defend; town left partially drained and in ruins [FW]; [IF] Basgiath records blame Poromish unrest; Navarre declared the area unfit for trade (BF). Trade Agreement of Resson moved elsewhere.

## 5. POROMIEL

### Cordyn
- Southern-coast city of Krovla; home of Viscount Tecarus; Syrena and Catriona Cordella live here; Xaden once had a room in the palace during his engagement to Catriona (ER).
- Palace on the farthest eastern peak: white pillars and marble, blue pools on five terraces stepping down a gentle hillside above the beach so it seems to glow; ornate gardens and butterfly garden; guards in crimson; gaming arena cut into the hillside from magic-drained Braevick stone; sea shifts turquoise to aqua; hot climate (ER, BF).
- Travel: ~2 days from Basgiath; 10-12 h from Aretia. Jumping-off port for the Isles [OS].
- Scenes: Xaden's betrothal visits, Tecarus' schemes [IF/OS]. Game idea: sun-drenched palace with intrigue/tournament arena.

### Draithus
- Coastal city, Krovla, second most populous, ruled by Viscount Tecarus (ER). Raised plateau behind high walls; central spiralling Spiral Tower housing the armory (BF). Amber fields and pale beaches around (ER). Medaro Pass connects it to Tyrrendor via granite cliffs (BF).
- Scenes: [OS] Battle of Draithus - venin led by Theophanie attack; refugees at Medaro Pass; Violet kills Theophanie with a Dunne-blessed dagger; Imogen and Dain defend the armory; Quinn is mortally wounded (BF; (?) details). Esben airspace route between Sumerton and Draithus (ER).
- Game idea: besieged-city defence with tower armory.

### Zolya, Braevick, Cliffsbane, Anca
- Braevick Province: central Poromiel, ruled by Viscount Tecarus (ER); Esben-bounded (BF); source of magic-bearing Braevick stone.
- Zolya: Poromiel's third-largest city (ER; BF says second-largest, conflict); ~4 h flight from Navarre's borders (ER); Battle of Zolya - two-day fight, city fell to venin and wyverns (ER).
- Cliffsbane: gryphon flier academy in Zolya (ER); fell to the venin (BF). Also a landmark listed in Anca.
- Anca: city beside the Stonewater River, near Zolya; abandoned after venin attacks in 634 AU (ER, BF). No visuals.

### Xortrys
- Capital of Zehyllna (see below), not Poromiel (?) - one search snippet confused it; BF map and FF agree it is Zehyllna's capital.

### Gianfar
- Late battle where gryphons and dragons fight together against the Barrens' army [OS?/later] (ER; location unknown). Terrain unknown (?).

### Leothan's remote isle / Barrens
- The Barrens: east of Poromiel; drained by the venin; no landmarks (BF).

## 6. ISLE KINGDOMS [OS]

General: restrict visitors and refuse Navarrians; trade with Poromiel in luxuries such as Deverelli silk (ER). Most have no magic, so colours look faded to riders (ER recap). Each isle has an entry trial (FF/BG).

### Deverelli (southernmost)
- Capital Matyas; King Courtlyn IV; neutral, no gods, trade in goods and secrets (ER/BG). Armed by rank (BG).
- Look: long white beaches, turquoise water, soft green hills with stonework, hot tropical; colours muted (FF, BF).
- Matyas: grows under a canopy of massive trees (patchy shade), four main piers, shops, tea house, tavern, dressmaker, large market, gold-and-spice market to the south, financial district on the hill (FF); narrow lanes climb to palaces (BF). Small barrier island to the east with grand residences with private docks (BF).
- King's palace: carved into hillside, gray columns at entrance, four-storey open-air structure with minimal walls, dining hall under palms, ~100-yard dragon meadow, courtyards lit by blue glass orbs holding glowing moths, white-clad guards, pure-white panthers (BF, FF).
- Tomes and Tales: two-storey bookshop with tall shelves, big windows, worn armchairs, smell of old paper (BF).
- Tecarus' estate: two-storey manor on the northern coast ~10 miles east of Matyas, white columns, thick stone walls, palm-lined paths, game-rich valleys south (BF).
- Dress: flowing white or gold tunics, pastel gowns. Dolphins in the sea.

### Unnbriel (Isle of Dunne)
- Capital Eistol; Queen Marlis; goddess of war Dunne; blood offering, cowardice the worst sin; dedicates have silver hair (BG, search snippets). ~9 h dragon west of Deverelli (BF map, fan).
- Look: rocky beaches with a narrow sand strip and boulders; thick muted-green hills; hot, stifling; no magic (FF).
- Eistol is heavily fortified, walls built so a few hold off many; port city Soneram; swivelling cross-bolts around the isle (FF).
- Trial: strongest warrior from each side duels in the plaza outside Dunne's temple, stones stained dark; High Priestess cuts forearms; agree on a single weapon; win = audience with the queen (FF; BG says seven-step trial by combat - conflict (?)).

### Hedotis (Isle of Hedeon)
- Capital Vidirys; Triumvirate (Faris, Roslyn, Nairi) elected; god of wisdom; no army; ~13 h from Unnbriel; cooler; paler vegetation (BF, FF, BG).
- Vidirys: coastal cream-stone city, no harbour; boats rest on sandy patches of rocky shore, large ships anchor offshore; wide wooden walkway beach to market; neighbourhoods around shared market squares; three-storey matching buildings with evenly spaced windows and muted flower baskets (BF). Rebuilt about 150 years ago on a planned grid; libraries and colleges (FF).
- Faris and Talia's house: northeast shore, stone patio over the coast, guest rooms three storeys up, golden doorknobs, cream hallway (BF).
- People: many purple eyes from the mineral viladrite; pastel off-shoulder tunics, metallic embroidery = wealth (BF).
- Trial: three robed triumvirs with jade stones question the applicant; all three must set stones on the table (FF).

### Zehyllna (Isle of Zihnal/Zinhal)
- Capital Xortrys (spelled Xortyrs in one source); god of luck; ruled by a queen; only isle with magic, a faint current enough to sense bonds but not to channel (FF, BG); language Zehylish. Far northwest beyond trade routes, via an archipelago.
- Look: white sand beaches, green-leafed trees, sparkling rivers, waterfalls (FF, BF).
- Xortrys: city ringed by a curved waterfall with rivers splitting off, appearing as an island; walls rising from water; jumbled new-on-old architecture; district with a big amphitheatre (BF).
- Trial: in the amphitheatre, with a table, one chair and a canvas bag of gift cards; you accept the drawn gift without complaint (FF). Scenes [OS]: Calixta's greeting ritual; Trager's gift foreshadows his arrow death; the queen pledges soldiers (BF; card game for an army of 40,000 (search snippet)).

### Loysam
- God of love Loial; guards but no army; farthest isle (BG, ER recap). No capital named.

### Other isle notes
- Minor isles beyond Zehyllna, northwest (BF map). Cerlian Sea (ER recap).
