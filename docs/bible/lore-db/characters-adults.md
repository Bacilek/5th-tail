# Lore DB: Adults, non-cadet characters, minor dragons, Broccoli

Scope: Empyrean series (Fourth Wing [FW], Iron Flame [IF], Onyx Storm [OS]). Spoilers included. All text paraphrased.
Legend: (?) = uncertain or sources conflict; source site is named in brackets. Sources abbreviated: ER = empyreanriders.com, BG = bookishgoblin.com, I&I = inkandimaginings.com, BSR = bookseriesrecaps.com, RC = recaptains.co.uk, SR = screenrant.com.
Many wiki pages say "appearance not described"; where so, left blank rather than invented. The `[Game note]` lines are design suggestions, not canon.

## 1. The Sorrengail family

### General Lilith Sorrengail ("The Flame of Basgiath")
- **Title (RESOLVED, with a caveat):** Her rank is **General**; she is the commanding general of Basgiath War College (BG: "Commander General of Basgiath"; ER: General, commanding officer of the college). **Commandant** is a different post: Colonel **Lyron Panchek** is Commandant of the Riders Quadrant in IF [ER Panchek page, BG, I&I]. A fan analysis page (storgy.com) calls Lilith "Commandant"; treat as loose usage (?). Whether she also held the Commandant title in FW is not settled by the web sources (?) - book reader should confirm.
- **Appearance:** tall, strong, muscular; golden-brown hair cut short and practical; stern, arrogant expression, imposing presence [ER]. Eye colour not stated online (BG: "unknown").
- **Dragon:** Aimsir ("weather"), brown, tail type not stated anywhere (?). Aimsir dies with her in IF.
- **Signet:** storm wielder (weather, lightning, large storms).
- **Personality:** cold, disciplined, calculating, feared and respected; deeply loves her children but shows it harshly. Softer and kinder while her husband lived; his death hardened her. Loyal to Navarre but morally complicated: she argued against Melgren's brutality and against forcing rebel children to watch their parents' executions.
- **Relationships:** husband Asher (scribe, died); children Mira, Brennan, Violet. Believed a fever in pregnancy caused Violet's fragile body. Captured Fen Riorson in the rebellion and branded him "the Great Betrayer". Made the deal with Xaden over the 107 rebel-children conscripts. Close adviser: Colonel Aetos (who later betrays and replaces her).
- **Arc:** [FW] Violet's formidable mother, sending her to the Riders Quadrant instead of the Scribes; part of the leadership hiding the truth about the venin. [IF] Reveals/defends secrets, sends Brennan to war knowing of venin; sacrifices herself and Aimsir to feed the Basgiath wardstone (with Sloane's siphoning) to protect her children. [OS] Her death shapes everyone; Aetos takes over as Commanding General of Basgiath.

### Asher Sorrengail (a.k.a. Asher Daxton as author name)
- Scribe, Violet's father. Appearance not described online; Brennan shares his nose [ER]. Valued history, languages, poisons; set puzzles for Violet. Published under two surnames on the isles, feathertails, and the second Krovlan uprising. Took Violet to an isle to dedicate her to Dunne; the dedication was never completed [ER]. Left books for Violet with a merchant in Deverelli. Listed dead of heart failure; fans speculate on a cover-up (unconfirmed).

### Mira Sorrengail
- Violet's older sister; rider (graduated top of class), Lieutenant in FW, Captain in IF.
- **Appearance:** tall, strong, muscular build; golden-brown hair cut short like their mother's. Scars: one from Teine at her Threshing; a long one from earlobe to collarbone from Montserrat [ER].
- **Dragon:** Teine ("fire"), **green clubtail** (sources agree [I&I, ER, BG, SparkNotes-style lists]). Signet: extending wards (shielding).
- **Personality:** disciplined, shrewd, can be cruel, but fiercely protective of Violet; a gifted rider. Resents perceived parental favouritism towards Violet.
- **Arc:** [FW] stationed at Montserrat, helps Violet visit Rhiannon's family; ordered Violet's survival vest made from Teine's scales. [IF] Threatens her mother to see the wardstone, deserts to the rebellion; learns Lilith lied for years; strained with Brennan. [OS] Grows; investigates family secrets (grandmother Niara); on the quest squad (her gift at Zehyllna is wine); captured by Theophanie at Draithus, throat slit, saved by Brennan with Sloane's help.
- Note: the ER Onyx Storm guide page mislists Mira's dragon as Breugan; that is a table error (Breugan is Prof. Carr's). Ignore.

### Brennan Sorrengail
- Violet's older brother; mender (exceptionally rare); dragon Marbh ("dead"), **orange daggertail**.
- **Appearance:** close-cropped reddish-brown curls, dimple when he grins, angular features with crinkles at the eyes, nose like their father's [ER].
- **Personality:** kind, sharp strategist, practical; wrote the survival guide for Violet (the "Book of Brennan").
- **Arc:** [FW] presumed killed at the Battle of Aretia (Fen's arrow); Naolin failed to save him by siphoning; final chapter reveals he lives in hiding in Aretia, close to Xaden. [IF] Open as Lt. Colonel in Aretia; heals Violet after venin poison at Athebyne. [OS] Reclaims the Sorrengail name; heals Mira after Theophanie.

## 2. Basgiath leadership and staff

### General Augustine Melgren
- Commanding general of all Navarre. **Dragon:** Codagh, black. **Signet:** battle foresight (sees battle outcomes); blind spot: cannot see outcomes when three or more "marked ones" are involved [ER].
- **Appearance:** cold, calculating eyes; uniform covered with medals; unsettling presence [ER]. Hair/age not described.
- **Personality:** menacing, cold; had rebel leaders executed by Codagh's fire.
- **Arc:** [FW] sinister; Violet jokes he might be venin under a mask. [IF] Xaden exploits his blind spot. [OS] **Conflict:** one recap (RC) calls him a "Sage" with battle foresight who warns Xaden not to fly into battle (?). ER's OS page lists Deigh (red daggertail) as his dragon, which looks like a table error (Deigh is Liam's). BSR calls Colonel Aetos "the new general" in OS. Treat Melgren's status in OS as UNRESOLVED.

### Colonel (later General) Aetos
- Dain's father; adviser to Lilith. Warm brown eyes and thick brows like Dain [ER]. Friendly mask in FW; sent cadets to fight venin under the cover of War Games; in IF works with venin and orders attacks on Violet; becomes Commanding General of Basgiath in OS and disowns Dain. Dragon not stated (?).

### Colonel Lyron Panchek
- Commandant of the Riders Quadrant; brown eyes; **green clubtail** dragon (unnamed) [ER, BG, I&I]. Seen as ambitious (wants Lilith's, then Melgren's post). [OS] revealed as a traitor feeding the venin in exchange for access to his son; Berwyn kills his netted dragon with an alloy dagger and turns on him.

### Major Burton Varrish (spelled Varrish; "Varish" is a typo)
- Vice Commandant of Basgiath from IF; reassigned after three prisoners died during his interrogations. Signet: sees weakness. Dragon: Solas ("light"), **orange daggertail**. Reports to Panchek; friend of Colonel Aetos.
- **Appearance:** thick black hair always neatly combed, spotless boots, cruel smile [ER].
- **Personality:** severe, ruthless, torturer; pushes Violet to cast lightning to near burnout; Solas burns third-year cadets. ER wiki lists his status dead; how/when is unknown (?).

### Jack Barlowe (cadet in FW, venin in IF/OS)
- **Appearance:** big, imposing; glacial blue eyes, thick blond brows, cruel grin; red-rimmed eyes when venin.
- **Signet:** pain projection. **Dragon:** Baide ("herd"), **orange scorpiontail**, aggressive; four previous riders; chose Jack at Threshing.
- **Arc:** [FW] pushes a candidate off the parapet, vows to kill Violet; defeated by oranges (he is allergic); presumed dead in the War Games landslide. [IF] Secretly healed by Nolon; revealed venin; kills Baide in the ward chamber, collapsing Navarre's wards; imprisoned and sedated by serum. [OS] Remains prisoner; used as bargaining chip for Mira. Berwyn calls him son.

### Colonel Nolon Colbersy and Winifred
- Nolon: 84 years old, Lt. Colonel then Colonel; mender; the healer who has treated Violet for years. Brown skin, bright white teeth [ER]. Kind to Violet; disagrees with conscripting rebel children; leaves a window open during the squad challenge, yet reports Violet after she steals the ward journals. [IF] Spends months trying to heal Jack's soul; reports to Lilith that there is no cure, only control. Dragon not stated (?). Winifred is his wife; no more detail online.

### Professors (confirmed)
- **Colonel Lewis Markham:** Head of Scribes and history professor; curator of the Scribes Quadrant; wrote "Navarre: An Unedited History"; strict, shrewd, treats Violet as star pupil/successor. Appearance not described (cream-coloured attire in a photo). Violet suspects him of hiding venin facts.
- **Colonel Kaori:** Professor of Dragonkind; illusionist signet; dark eyes and a neat moustache; enthusiastic, well-liked; dragon Smachd ("discipline"), colour/tail unknown. Demonstrates dragon temperament with a Red Scorpiontail illusion (Ghrian).
- **Major Devera:** Battle Brief professor; short hair, black brows, deep brown skin, many medals, purple Flame Section path; stern; later joins the resistance at Aretia; fresh arm wound in IF. BG: purple hair, unnamed red clubtail (?). Signet unknown.
- **Professor Carr:** signet fire; white hair, imposing and cold; trains Violet's lightning; dragon Breugan ("lies"), **orange daggertail**. Warns that Violet and Xaden would seem a threat to leadership.
- **Professor Emetterio:** combat professor; shaved head, dark brow, thick hands, compact frame.
- **Professor/Captain Grady:** tall, excellent posture, authoritative; RSC (Rider Survival Course) professor in IF; in OS leads the Senarium search squad and is accidentally killed by Aura on Deverelli [BSR]. Signet unknown.
- **Quade, Kallista:** no confirmed web source found. UNVERIFIED; do not use.

### King Tauri of Navarre
- Toothy smile, moustache, a uniform of medals he never earned [ER]; green Tauri eyes (BG). Image-conscious, showy; wards only what is worth showing off. Three sons: Halden, Alic, Aaric (Cam Graycastle/Tauri); one son died at Threshing. No dragon or signet known (probably not a rider). Royal vault at Basgiath.

### Prince Halden Tauri
- Firstborn, heir; Violet's ex. Handsome, sharp cheekbones, smirk, summer-green eyes; a little shorter than Aaric; sharp blue uniforms. Charming, witty, jealous and manipulative; grieves his twin. [OS] arrives at Basgiath, joins the quest to the isles, is detained on Deverelli, replaced by Aaric; later stabbed (RC: by King Courtlyn in Deverelli (?)).

## 3. Rebels, Poromiel and the isles

### Fen Riorson
- Xaden's father; infantry quadrant (not rider); charismatic orator; led the rebellion; died under interrogation accusing the kings of a conspiracy; last words redacted. Appearance not described online. Hoped Xaden would join the infantry.

### Talia Riorson
- Xaden's mother. Tall, regal, long black hair, dark brown gold-flecked eyes (Xaden's), high cheekbones. Left after the marriage contract and married Feris of the Hedotis triumvirate; sons Simeon and Gaius. [OS] reunion on Hedotis; Xaden cold; she defends him; a poisoned cake nearly kills Garrick (the husband is blamed [BSR]).

### Naolin
- Tairn's previous rider; siphoner; died at the Battle of Aretia trying to save Brennan. Looks not described. Fan theories only (turned venin / revived Brennan).

### Viscount Tecarus (also called Duke in one site (?))
- Of Cordyn, Poromiel; Cat and Syrena's uncle; named heir by Queen Maraya, king after she is killed in Suniva. Tall, dark-haired, blue tunic with gold brocade, flushed round cheeks, very white teeth. Signet: senses what people most desire. Collector, manipulative; bargained the luminary for Violet's gryphon training [IF]; as king trades library access for Poromish cadets at Basgiath and helps the quest reach Deverelli [OS]. Source conflict: first in line vs second (?).

### Catriona "Cat" Cordella
- Gryphon flier (Cliffsbane), third in line to the Poromish throne, Xaden's ex-fiancee (engaged about nine months). Beautiful, slender, glossy black hair in a braid, fair skin, often wearing purple. Signet: heightens emotions. Gryphon: Kiralair ("Kira"). Rival-then-ally of Violet; [OS] starts a relationship with Trager.
- **Syrena Cordella:** Cat's older sister, black hair, second in line, gryphon rider at Resson.
- **Trager:** second-year flier with gryphon Sila; Cat's partner; killed by an arrow (his Zihnal gift) on Zehyllna; Sila dies with him [RC].
- **Maren:** petite flier, Cat's best friend; her parents were killed by Theophanie.

### Drake Cordella
- Captain, flier from Poromiel; Cat and Syrena's cousin; nephew of Tecarus; author of the venin compendium on dark wielders and "The Nightwing Drift"; wry humour [ER]. Appearance not described online. [OS] one of six riders chosen for Violet's quest squad. **Yes: he is Broccoli's owner (see 5).**

### Theophanie
- Venin Maven, former priestess of Dunne (worn tattoo on forehead). Long silver hair in a braid, dark purple robes, red veins round eyes, chapped lips. Storm/lightning wielder. Wants Violet to become venin; killed Maren's parents; rides a huge wyvern (BSR/ER guide). Dislikes Berwyn. Killed by Violet with Dunne's dagger from Aaric [OS].

### Berwyn
- Venin Sage/Maven, sire of Xaden and Jack Barlowe (called Jack his son). Long scar down his face (from Xaden throwing him in a ravine). Kills dragons with an alloy dagger. Xaden cannot kill him because of the sire bond. Identity guesses (Alic, Naolin, etc.) are fan theories.

### Dunne
- Goddess of War and Strength; priestesses; Asher tried to dedicate Violet to her.

## 5. Broccoli the kitten [OS]

- **Where/when:** on the isle of Zehyllna, during Zihnal's card-game "gifts" ceremony, in the stretch of OS before the irid storyline (placed near ch. 40 by the ER read-along, which describes the island as orange-coloured).
- **Who receives it:** Drake Cordella. Gifts to others: Mira wine, Maren tunics, Dain a slap, Garrick a rusted bucket, Aaric a broken mirror, Xaden an empty glass box, Violet a broken compass, Cat a ruby necklace, Ridoc a kiss, Trager a fatal arrow [ER, RC]. SR says the locals called the kitten "the claw."
- **Giver:** a large "bear of a man" per reader comments (?), unnamed.
- **Naming:** Drake names it Broccoli. A Goodreads highlight suggests he jokes with Mira, who proposed "Carrots" because it is orange, and he picks the name to annoy her (?, jumbled excerpt).
- **Gender:** read-along says "her"; SR says "him" (?).
- **Fate:** not shown again; author says safe: Rebecca Yarros told the Fantasy Fangirls Pod she will not kill a cat (SR). ER Q&A: Broccoli is based on her real cat Boots. A blog post claims Broccoli is with Drake in Cordyn (secondary).
