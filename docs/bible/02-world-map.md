# 02 - World map (Navarre, Poromiel and beyond)

Sources: Empyrean Riders wiki (ER), Fandom wiki, Literary Lifestyle map guides (LL). Paraphrased. `(?)` = uncertain or conflicting; `(M)` = from memory, unverified; `(invented)` = our own filler needed for gameplay.

## Continent overview
- **Navarre** (west) - kingdom ruled by King Tauri (ER). Mountains rich in ore and timber, deer, elk.
- **Poromiel** (east) - kingdom; ruler listed as Viscount Tecarus (ER) (?). Three provinces.
- **Isle Kingdoms** - five large and thirteen smaller islands around the continent, former Great War allies (ER). Needed for Onyx Storm content (DREAM).
- Border: Navarre closed borders after the war; Poromiel refugees were allowed to stay under the wards for a limited time (ER). Trade Agreement of Resson links the kingdoms.

## Natural features
Cliffs of Dralor (>12,000 ft, Arctile Ocean to the Tyrrendor plateau), Esben Mountains (Sipene village), Iakobos River, the Vale (dragon home / hatching grounds, LL), the Barrens (hostile army source (?)).

## Game-map plan (our scale)
The playable continent is stylised and compressed (travel by dragon ~ minutes). Zones in order of building (see TODO phases):
1. **Morraine home region** (Phase 3): Basgiath (full), Vale, Chantara village, Montserrat outpost, river, forests, mountain pass.
2. **Tyrrendor** (Phase 3/5): Aretia, Athebyne, Resson, Dralor cliffs, plains.
3. **Poromiel border** (Phase 5): Krovla/Braevick borderlands, Zolya, Cordyn (DREAM).
4. **Calldyr / capital area** (DREAM).
5. **Isles** (DREAM, Onyx Storm era).

Existing prototype map (580 x 580 m): lower college, Healers/Infantry/Scribe quadrants, Citadel mesa with Gauntlet and Flight Field, Conscription Tower + Parapet, Basgiath Village, Venin Camp. This becomes the Basgiath core of the larger world.
