# 01 - Lore canon (summary + rules)

Detailed running notes live in `docs/lore.md` (early research, partner decisions). This file is the structured canon for design. Sources are fan wikis only (we cannot read the books); the owners are the final authority.

## Lore handling rules
1. Terminology strictly from the books; **no Harry Potter dragons**. The broom is the only HP element.
2. Facts are paraphrased; `(?)` = uncertain, `(M)` = from memory, `(invented)` = ours.
3. Dialogue/quest text is original; no book quotes.
4. When sources conflict, record the conflict and ask the partner (`99-open-questions.md`).
5. Canon characters keep canon fates; the player is an original cadet.

## Setting in brief
- Navarre (west) and Poromiel (east) across a border; Navarre is protected by **wards** powered through dragon bonds; they fail at times and demand sacrifices. See 02-world-map.
- **Basgiath War College** trains Riders (dragons), Infantry, Healers and Scribes. Riders cross the **Parapet**; first-years face the **Gauntlet**; the **Threshing** bonds dragons. See 03-locations.
- **Venin**: humans who corrupted their souls by drawing magic directly from the earth; ranks Initiate, Asim, Sage, Maven; drain life; immune to dragon fire; control **wyverns** (killing the venin kills their wyverns). See 08.
- **Signets**: bond-born magical abilities unique to each rider. See 08.
- **Gryphons and fliers**: allied air force; relation to the wardstone is a plot element.
- **Gods and the Unnamed**: sources unreadable; do not build mechanics on them until verified.
- **Dragonglass/ore, the Rift**: not found in readable sources; do not use.

## Timeline anchors
Conscription Day mid-July (ER). Reunification Day is 1 July (Battle of Aretia commemoration, ER). Basgiath program is three years; first-years have no family contact; ~a quarter graduate. Parapet ~18 in wide, ~200 ft high, 15-20% fall (ER).

## College structure facts
Four wings x three sections (Flame, Claw, Tail) x three squads; third-years lead wings/sections; failure to pass the written exam and agility test sends cadets to Infantry; quadrants have own campuses; Riders citadel entrance is the Parapet.
