# 05 - Factions, institutions and standing

| Faction | Description | Player relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Quadrant culture (for animation, NPC lines, music, costumes)
- Riders: arrogant, elite, grim humour; colours red/black; leather and scale-accent uniforms.
- Infantry: disciplined, practical; brown/grey; chainmail/plate.
- Healers: calm, practical; green/white; satchels, herb bundles.
- Scribes: reserved, observant; violet/charcoal; ink-stained hands, many pockets.

## Standing and ranks
- Wing standing (Riders): cadet rank -> squad member -> squad leader -> section leader -> wingleader (aspirational).
- Quadrant standing for non-Riders.
- Reputation values per faction (-100..100) affect prices, access, dialogue, guard behaviour.
- Crimes: assault, theft, trespass in restricted areas (Archives vaults), duelling rules.

## Faction quest lines (see 07-quests)
Each quadrant has its own 8-12 quest chain from first day to graduation/third-year role; Riders' main line follows the books' timeline.

## Politics hooks
Tension between loyalists and marked ones, Navarre closed borders, wards failing, Venin infiltrations, Tyrrish independence, dragon-human relations. Player choices shift small local outcomes; major story beats stay canon to keep lore fidelity.
