## World
- Continent: Navarre (main kingdom; Basgiath is capital, in province Morraine), Poromiel, Tyrrendor (rebel province, Riorson clan) (?), the Barrens.
- Basgiath War College: four quadrants – Riders, Scribes, Healers, Infantry. Rider path: Parapet crossing, Threshing (dragon bonding), Gauntlet, wings/squads, Archives (Scribes' library).
## Dragons

- Known: Tairn (black daggertail, Violet), Andarna (gold feathertail), Sgaeyl (blue daggertail, Xaden), Teine (green clubtail), Aotrom (brown swordtail, Ridoc) (?).
- Riders have signets (personal magic) channelled via the bond.
