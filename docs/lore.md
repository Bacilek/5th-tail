# Lore notes (Fourth Wing / Empyrean)
Sources: fan wikis/guides (empyreanriders.com, the-empyrean-series.fandom.com, thebookfeed.com, screenrant, sparknotes, Wikipedia). They disagree in places; items marked (?) are uncertain. **Fidelity to the books is the top priority**; when the user (or partner, who has read them) corrects something, that wins.

## Basgiath War College (layout, per fan wikis)
- Capital of Navarre (province Morraine). Split into a **lower college** (Scribe, Healer, Infantry and Administrative areas) and the **Citadel** (Riders Quadrant) on the ridgeline high above.
- Every quadrant has its OWN campus: Infantry (largest, over 1000 cadets, ground level, reached via the **northern turret**), Healers (southern part, **southern gate**), Scribes + **Archives** (below ground, reached by a **central tunnel**) (?), Riders (Citadel).
- **Gauntlet**: built into the ridgeline between the Citadel and the **flight field** / the Vale.
## Conscription Day (mid/late July; sources vary)
- Candidates who pass written + physical exam choose any quadrant; failures go to Infantry. Children of rebels ("marked") are forced into Riders (as Xaden).
- Scribes record everyone, including cadets crossing the Parapet.
- **Only Riders cross the Parapet.** Many die (sources: ~15-20% of Rider candidates (?)).
## The Parapet
- Narrow stone span, ~200 ft (~60 m) above the lower college, ~18 inches wide (?), no railing; wind and rain/mist make it slick. Leads from the main college (a high tower) to the Riders Quadrant Citadel. Ends at the Citadel courtyard with a ~12 ft drop (?); Citadel walls ~10 ft thick, 8 ft tall (?).
## Riders Quadrant structure
- 4 **Wings** (First-Fourth), each with 3 **Sections** (Flame, Claw, Tail), each with 3 **Squads** (1-3). Wingleaders usually third-years. Violet: Fourth Wing, wingleader Xaden, squad leader Dain; Cianna: XO of Flame Section, Fourth Wing.
- Riders: dragon bond at the Threshing, signets (personal magic).
## World
- Continent: Navarre, Poromiel, Tyrrendor (rebel province, Riorson clan) (?), the Barrens.
